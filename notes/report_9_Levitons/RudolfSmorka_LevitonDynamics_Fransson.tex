\section{Setup}
A Lorentzian time-dependent voltage pulse
\begin{equation}
	V_L(t) =\frac{1}{\pi} \frac{2\tau}{(t-t^*)^2 + \tau^2}.
\end{equation}
with quantized flux 
\begin{equation}
	\phi = e\int_{-\infty}^\infty V(t)\dif t = 2\pi n
\end{equation}
generates a minimal excitation with an integer number $n$ of purely electronic states, so-called Leviton quasiparticles, above the Fermi sea without electron-hole pairs \cite{levitov1996electron}. Levitons have been observed experimentally in setups realizing two-dimensional quantum gases \cite{dubois2013minimal}. 
For $n=2$, which can be viewed as a unpolarized current of a spin-up and spin-down electron.  
We use $t^* = 40/t_0=\SI{241.8}{\femto\second}$ and $\tau = 7.5/t_0$. The voltage is applied by shifting the chemical potential of the left lead only. The parameters are inspired by the ones used in Franssons work
\begin{align}
	\varepsilon_0 &= \SI{0}{\milli\electronvolt}\\
	\omega_0 &= \SI{10}{\electronvolt}\\
	t_0 &= \SI{40}{\milli\electronvolt}\\
	t_1 &= \SI{4}{\milli\electronvolt}\\
	\lambda_0 &= \SI{1}{\milli\electronvolt}\\
	\lambda_1 &= \SI{0.1}{\milli\electronvolt}\\
	\Gamma &= \SI{10}{\milli\electronvolt}\\
	T &= \SI{25}{\milli\electronvolt}.
\end{align}
In the following, we will investigate the local population $n_i$, local spin polarization $s^z_i$ and the momentum-space population $n(\varepsilon_k)$ of single-particle eigenstate with energy $\varepsilon_k$ of the Hamiltonian at initial time. We consider several cases, outlined below:
\begin{itemize}
	\item[Set 1] $L=100$, $t_1=\lambda_0=\lambda_1 = \SI{0}{\electronvolt}$. This corresponds to a tight-binding chain with two electrons per site, or equivalently to two decoupled tight-binding chains for the two electron species due to absence of SOC. The spin-polarization $P$ of the left lead is varied between $0,\pm\frac{1}{2}$.
	\item[Set 2] $L=70$, $\lambda_0 = \SI{1}{\milli\electronvolt}$, $t_1=\lambda_1= \SI{0}{\electronvolt}$. This parameter set is used to investigate the influence of SOC on Leviton dynamics.
	\item[Set 3] $L=70$, $t_1 = \SI{4}{\milli\electronvolt}$, $\lambda_0=\lambda_1= \SI{0}{\electronvolt}$. This parameter set is used to investigate the influence of Peierls-coupling to classical bond vibrational modes on the Leviton dynamics.
	\item[Set 4] $L=70$, $t_1= \SI{4}{\milli\electronvolt}$, $\lambda_0=\SI{1}{\milli\electronvolt}$, $\lambda_1=\SI{0}{\electronvolt}$. This parameter set investigates the influence of both Peierls-coupling and SOC.
	\item[Set 5] $L=70$, Fransson parameters. This is the full Fransson model with the Parameters listed above.
\end{itemize}
